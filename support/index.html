Sharing bills with your partner

Open Settings → Share with your partner and send the invitation by Messages or Mail. When your partner taps it on their iPhone, Homeroll opens with your bills. Both of you need to be signed in to iCloud. Your partner doesn't need to buy Pro.

If changes don't show up on the other phone, check that iCloud is on for Homeroll under Settings → [your name] → iCloud on both phones, then open Homeroll on each.

Reminders

Choose when to be reminded for each bill, and the time of day in Settings. If reminders don't arrive, check that notifications are allowed for Homeroll in the iPhone Settings app.

Widgets

Touch and hold the Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit, then add a Homeroll widget. On the Upcoming widget, tap the circle next to a bill to mark it paid; Homeroll saves it, and shares it with your partner, the next time you open the app.

Importing bills

In Settings → Import bills from a CSV file, choose a file with a header row. Homeroll looks for columns named like name, amount, due date and repeat (in English, Danish, German, Swedish, Norwegian, Spanish or French), in any order. Rows it can't read are listed so you can fix them.

Purchases

Homeroll Pro is a one-time purchase. On a new device, open Settings → Restore purchase. With Family Sharing, your family gets Pro too.
