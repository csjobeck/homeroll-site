What it does

  • Shares your household's bills with your partner through iCloud, so you both see what's paid.
  • Shows a list of what's late and what's next, and a month calendar.
  • Reminds you before each bill, and lets you mark it paid from the notification or a widget.
  • Gets you started with the bills most households in your country pay.
  • Imports bills from a CSV file, for example from another bill app.
  • One price, paid once. No subscription.
